**Figure 1.** Overview of pramana. Training DCPs are characterized once, then explored through
LLM-guided rounds in which each design's analyst sees a draft knowledge base of the other
designs' results and the LLM's influence on run allocation is weighted by its measured
prediction accuracy. Discovered recipes are validated across designs before distillation into a
knowledge base, which is compiled for both LLM-based and deterministic optimization flows and
evaluated on unseen DCPs.
